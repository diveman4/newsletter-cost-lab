MailerLite vs beehiiv: what each costs at every list size

MailerLite is an email marketing tool priced by subscribers; beehiiv is a newsletter platform with a website built in. This page puts their published prices side by side from 500 to 100,000 subscribers. Prices are from each vendor's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-21.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free plans: MailerLite: Free up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month. beehiiv: Free up to 2,500 subscribers, 1 publication, beehiiv branding, no paid subscriptions.
  • Free newsletter: With yearly billing, the cheaper one for a free newsletter is MailerLite at 5,000, 10,000, 25,000 and 100,000; beehiiv at 500, 1,000, 2,500 and 50,000 subscribers.
  • Charging readers: MailerLite paid-newsletter costs aren't in our data yet, so this page compares free newsletters only.
  • Smallest paid plans: MailerLite Comfort starts at $10.80/month billed yearly for up to 500 subscribers; beehiiv Lite starts at $49/month billed yearly for up to 1,000.
