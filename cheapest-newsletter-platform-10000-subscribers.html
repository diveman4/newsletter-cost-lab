Cheapest newsletter platform for 10,000 subscribers

10,000 subscribers is where many free plans end and prices start to climb. This page ranks every platform we track by what it costs at exactly 10,000 subscribers, for a free newsletter and for a paid one. Prices verified on each vendor's pricing page on 2026-11-14.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free newsletter: Kit has a free plan that holds 10,000 subscribers, so the cheapest price is $0 a month. Substack is also $0 if you never charge.
  • Paid newsletter (500 readers paying $8/month): Kit Free is cheapest at $290/month including fees.
  • Growing past 10,000? At 12,000 subscribers the cheapest option becomes systeme.io Unlimited at $80.83/month. Check the next tier before you commit.
