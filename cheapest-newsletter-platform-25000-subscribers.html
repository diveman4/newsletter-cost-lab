Cheapest newsletter platform for 25,000 subscribers

25,000 subscribers is well past every capped free plan, so here the monthly bill depends entirely on each platform's paid tiers. This page ranks every platform we track by what it costs at exactly 25,000 subscribers, for a free newsletter and for a paid one. Prices verified on each vendor's pricing page on 2026-11-28.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free newsletter: systeme.io Unlimited is cheapest at $80.83/month (Substack is also $0 if you never charge).
  • Paid newsletter (1,250 readers paying $8/month): systeme.io Unlimited is cheapest at $746/month including fees.
  • Growing past 25,000? At 30,000 subscribers the cheapest option is systeme.io Unlimited at $80.83/month. systeme.io Unlimited has no contact cap in our data, so its price stays the same as you grow; the other platforms step up in tiers.
