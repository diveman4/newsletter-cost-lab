Ghost(Pro) pricing explained: what you'll pay at every list size

Ghost(Pro) prices depend on how many subscribers you have and on whether you pay yearly or monthly. This page lists every price on Ghost(Pro)'s own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-21.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

How much does Ghost(Pro) cost? There's no free plan. Paid plans start at $15/month billed yearly ($18 month to month) for up to 1,000 subscribers. With yearly billing, at 10,000 subscribers the cheapest option is $88/month (Publisher) and at 25,000 subscribers the cheapest option is $141/month (Publisher).

The short version

  • Free plan: No free plan on Ghost(Pro) (free trial available). Self-hosting Ghost is free but you run the server. Paid subscriptions need a paid plan.
  • Paid plans: Starter, Publisher, Business. The cheapest starts at $15/month billed yearly ($18 month to month) for up to 1,000 subscribers.
  • Yearly billing saves about 17% on the Starter plan, depending on list size.
  • Paid newsletters: Ghost(Pro) takes 0% of paid-subscription revenue; Stripe's standard 2.9% + $0.30 per payment still applies.
