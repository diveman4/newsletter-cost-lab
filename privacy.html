Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Newsletter Cost Lab is a small independent site. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we don't run ads.

What this site collects

  • No accounts, forms or newsletters. There is nothing to sign up for here.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts. We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or similar tools, and the site itself sets no cookies.
  • The cost calculator runs in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to run the service. We don't receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Search statistics

We use Google Search Console to see which search terms bring people to the site. It shows only totals (for example, how many times a page appeared in Google results), not who visited.

Affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links, marked with an asterisk (*): currently Kit, beehiiv, systeme.io. If you click one, you leave this site, and the platform or its affiliate partner may set a cookie on its own website to record that you came from us. That cookie is governed by that company's privacy policy, not ours. Read more in our affiliate disclosure.

Contact and GitHub

The contact page points to the site's public GitHub repository. Anything you post there is public and handled under GitHub's terms.
