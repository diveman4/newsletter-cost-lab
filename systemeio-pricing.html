systeme.io pricing explained: what you'll pay at every list size

systeme.io prices depend on how many contacts you have and on whether you pay yearly or monthly. This page lists every price on systeme.io's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-28.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

How much does systeme.io cost? It's free up to 2,000 contacts. Paid plans start at $14.17/month billed yearly ($17 month to month) for up to 5,000 contacts. With yearly billing, at 10,000 contacts the cheapest option is $39.17/month (Webinar) and at 25,000 contacts the cheapest option is $80.83/month (Unlimited).

The short version

  • Free plan: Free up to 2,000 contacts with unlimited emails; also includes funnels, courses and checkout. You can charge readers on the free plan.
  • Paid plans: Startup, Webinar, Unlimited. The cheapest starts at $14.17/month billed yearly ($17 month to month) for up to 5,000 contacts.
  • Yearly billing saves about 17% on the Startup plan, depending on list size.
  • Paid newsletters: systeme.io takes 0% of paid-subscription revenue; Stripe's standard 2.9% + $0.30 per payment still applies.
