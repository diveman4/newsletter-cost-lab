Kit (formerly ConvertKit) pricing explained: what you'll pay at every list size

Kit prices depend on how many subscribers you have and on whether you pay yearly or monthly. This page lists every price on Kit's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-28.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

How much does Kit cost? It's free up to 10,000 subscribers. Paid plans start at $33/month billed yearly ($39 month to month) for up to 1,000 subscribers. With yearly billing, at 10,000 subscribers the cheapest option is $0/month (Free) and at 25,000 subscribers the cheapest option is $166/month (Creator).

The short version

  • Free plan: Free up to 10,000 subscribers, 1 user, no visual automations. You can charge readers on the free plan.
  • Paid plans: Creator, Pro. The cheapest starts at $33/month billed yearly ($39 month to month) for up to 1,000 subscribers.
  • Yearly billing saves about 15-17% on the Creator plan, depending on list size.
  • Paid newsletters: Kit charges 3.5% + $0.30 per transaction on paid subscriptions.

Our hands-on test of Kit

Tested by TA on 2026-11-25, following our fixed-task test. Account: 14-day trial (started automatically at sign-up).

Sign-up to first issue sent12 minutes
Sign-up form or landing page builtYes, 4.5 minutes. Hosted landing page from the "College" template; double opt-in on by default; the confirmation email reached Gmail's main inbox
Where the test issue landedGmail: Spam; Outlook: Not tested; Yahoo: Not tested

3-email welcome sequence on the free planNot possible. Not on the Free plan: Kit's help page lists email sequences as a paid-plan (Creator and up) feature. During the trial the sequence editor did open

What went wrong:

  • The test issue landed in Gmail's spam folder. It was sent from a Gmail address through Kit's servers ("via n.convertkit.com"). The double opt-in confirmation email from the same address reached the main inbox.
  • To send, you have to press "Continue" at the bottom right of the publish screen, which was easy to miss.
  • Right after adding a subscriber by hand, the list still showed 0 until the page was refreshed.
  • Export is tucked under a "…" menu in a pop-up at the bottom of the screen.

There was no plan choice at sign-up: a 14-day trial of paid features started automatically. Kit filled in a US mailing address in the email footer, so no home address was needed. Emails carry a "Built with Kit" badge.
