Cheapest newsletter platform for 1,000 subscribers

1,000 subscribers is a common first-year goal, and at this size several platforms are still free. This page ranks every platform we track by what it costs at exactly 1,000 subscribers, for a free newsletter and for a paid one. Prices verified on each vendor's pricing page on 2026-11-14.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free newsletter: beehiiv, Kit and systeme.io all have free plans that hold 1,000 subscribers, so the cheapest price is $0 a month. Substack is also $0 if you never charge.
  • Paid newsletter (50 readers paying $8/month): systeme.io Free is cheapest at $26.60/month including fees.
  • Growing past 1,000? At 3,000 subscribers the cheapest option becomes Kit Free at $0/month. Free plans on beehiiv (up to 2,500) and systeme.io (up to 2,000) stop before that, while Kit's goes to 10,000, so check where you expect to be in a year.
