MailerLite vs Kit: what each costs at every list size

MailerLite and Kit (formerly ConvertKit) are both email tools many people start a newsletter with. This page puts their published prices side by side from 500 to 55,000 subscribers, using each vendor's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-21.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Up to 10,000 subscribers: Kit's free plan covers the list, so a free newsletter costs $0 on Kit. MailerLite's free plan stops at 250 subscribers; above that its cheapest plan is Comfort, from $10.80/month billed yearly.
  • Above 10,000 subscribers both are paid and, with yearly billing, the prices are close (at most $28.90 a month apart in our table). The cheaper one switches with each vendor's price tiers (yearly billing; see the month-to-month table too): MailerLite at 15,000, 20,000, 25,000, 30,000, 40,000, 45,000 and 50,000; Kit at 35,000 and 55,000 subscribers.
  • Charging readers: Kit allows paid subscriptions on every plan, including Free, with a 3.5% + $0.30 transaction fee. MailerLite's paid-newsletter costs aren't in our data yet, so this page compares free newsletters only.
  • Free plans aren't equal: Kit: Free up to 10,000 subscribers, 1 user, no visual automations. MailerLite: Free up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month.
