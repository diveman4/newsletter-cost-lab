Ghost(Pro) vs beehiiv: what each costs at every list size

Ghost(Pro) and beehiiv both take a 0% cut of paid subscriptions, so for a paid newsletter the difference comes down to the plan price at your list size. This page puts their published prices side by side, for a free newsletter and for one that charges readers. Prices are from each vendor's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-28.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free plans: Ghost(Pro): No free plan on Ghost(Pro) (free trial available). Self-hosting Ghost is free but you run the server. beehiiv: Free up to 2,500 subscribers, 1 publication, beehiiv branding, no paid subscriptions.
  • Free newsletter: With yearly billing, the cheaper one for a free newsletter is Ghost(Pro) at 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000 and 100,000; beehiiv at 1,000 and 2,500 subscribers.
  • Charging readers: Ghost(Pro) needs Publisher or Business for them; beehiiv needs Lite or Pro for them. Both take 0% of paid-subscription revenue; Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 per payment still applies. In our example (5% of readers paying $8/month), the cheaper one is Ghost(Pro) at 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000 and 100,000 subscribers.
  • Members vs subscribers: Ghost(Pro) prices by members (free and paying sign-ups together); beehiiv prices by subscribers. Both count everyone on your list.
  • Ghost(Pro) Starter ($15/month billed yearly, up to 1,000 members) is the cheapest Ghost(Pro) plan but doesn't include paid memberships; Publisher and Business do. Ghost's pricing page shows Business at its 10,000-member price for smaller lists.
