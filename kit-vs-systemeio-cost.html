Kit vs systeme.io: what each costs at every list size

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) and systeme.io both let you charge readers on their free plans, but they price paid plans very differently: Kit steps up in subscriber tiers, while systeme.io has 3 plans set by contact count, the largest with no cap in our data. This page puts their published prices side by side from 1,000 to 50,000 subscribers. Prices are from each vendor's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-28.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free plans: Kit: Free up to 10,000 subscribers, 1 user, no visual automations. systeme.io: Free up to 2,000 contacts with unlimited emails; also includes funnels, courses and checkout.
  • Free newsletter: With yearly billing, the cheaper one for a free newsletter is Kit at 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000; systeme.io at 15,000, 25,000 and 50,000 subscribers.
  • Charging readers: Kit allows them on its free plan; systeme.io allows them on its free plan. Kit charges 3.5% + $0.30 per transaction; systeme.io takes 0% (Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 per payment still applies). In our example (5% of readers paying $8/month), the cheaper one is Kit at 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000; systeme.io at 1,000, 2,000, 15,000, 25,000 and 50,000 subscribers.
  • Both free up to 2,000: for a free newsletter, both cost $0 up to 2,000 subscribers. Past that, Kit's free plan continues to 10,000, while systeme.io moves to Startup ($14.17/month billed yearly, up to 5,000 contacts).
  • Past Kit's free plan: Kit Creator is $141/month billed yearly up to 15,000 subscribers and $316 up to 55,000 (the largest size in our data). systeme.io Unlimited stays at $80.83/month billed yearly, with no contact cap in our data.
  • Contacts vs subscribers: systeme.io prices by contacts (everyone on your list); Kit prices by subscribers. This page treats the two numbers as the same.
  • systeme.io yearly prices are calculated from its "2 months free" offer on yearly billing.
