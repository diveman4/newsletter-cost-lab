Kit vs beehiiv: what each costs at every list size

Kit and beehiiv are both popular with people starting a newsletter, but they price very differently. This page puts their published prices side by side at each list size, for a free newsletter and for one that charges readers. Prices verified on each vendor's pricing page on 2026-11-14.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free plans: Kit is free up to 10,000 subscribers; beehiiv is free up to 2,500. Between those sizes, a free newsletter costs $0 on Kit but needs a paid beehiiv plan.
  • Charging readers: Kit's free plan allows paid subscriptions with a 3.5% + $0.30 transaction fee. beehiiv needs a paid plan (Lite or above) but takes a 0% cut, so you pay only Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30.
  • In our paid-newsletter example, beehiiv is first the cheaper option at 15,000 subscribers ($568 vs $576 a month).

Things the price tables don't show

  • Kit sometimes runs a first-year discount on Pro. The tables use the standard price.
