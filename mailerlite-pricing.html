MailerLite pricing explained: what you'll pay at every list size

MailerLite prices depend on your subscriber count and on whether you pay yearly or monthly. This page lists every price on MailerLite's own pricing page, checked on 2026-11-14.

Links marked * are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. It never changes the numbers. Details.

The short version

  • Free plan: Free up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month.
  • Paid plans: Comfort, Power. The cheapest starts at $10.80/month billed yearly ($12 month to month) for up to 500 subscribers.
  • Yearly billing saves about 10% on the Comfort plan, depending on list size.
  • Paid newsletters: Paid-newsletter fees not compared yet.
