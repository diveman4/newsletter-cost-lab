Substack fees: when does the 10% cut cost more than a flat plan?

Substack is free until you charge readers, then keeps 10% of paid-subscription revenue on top of card fees. Flat-price platforms with a 0% cut (beehiiv Lite/Pro, Ghost Publisher) cost money every month but take nothing extra. This page finds the break-even point.

How to read this

The rule of thumb: Substack's cut overtakes a flat plan once 10% of your paid revenue is bigger than that plan's monthly price. At $8 per reader, every 10 paid subscribers adds $8 a month to Substack's take. A $49 plan is overtaken at around 62 paid readers.

Price isn't the only factor. Substack's network can bring readers, and moving paid subscribers off a platform later can mean asking them to re-subscribe. If you expect to charge readers within a year, run your own numbers in the calculator before you start.
