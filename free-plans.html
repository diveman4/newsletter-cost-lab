Which free plan fits?

  • You want the most room before paying: Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers, the highest fixed cap here. Substack has no cap, but takes 10% when you charge.
  • You want to sell or charge from day one without a monthly bill: Substack (10% cut), Kit (3.5% + $0.30 per transaction) and systeme.io (0% platform fee, up to 2,000 contacts) allow it on free plans.
  • You only need a small list for now: beehiiv (2,500) also works, but paid subscriptions need a paid plan.
